Title
SequenceCompressor — consensus-labeled work segments

Notes / Description
SequenceCompressor turns a public ordered list into assignable contiguous work segments. First time: open the linked StudioNet contract in GenLayer Studio, connect a test wallet, and call compile_sequence with a unique key, labels_json (for example ["Preparation","Execution","Verification"]), entries_json (a JSON list of 2–30 short steps), and a policy explaining how to label each step. Validators independently assign one closed label index per entry; the contract checks agreement and deterministically merges adjacent equal labels. Read get_compilation and get_segment to inspect the result. The owner can assign each segment to a distinct wallet, each operator acknowledges, and the owner seals after all acknowledgments. This records a workflow, not proof that work happened; no funds or external evidence. Audit: GenVM lint/typecheck, 7 direct tests, 5-validator GLSim, and finalized StudioNet deployment/AI write with exact source rechecked on Oct 8.
