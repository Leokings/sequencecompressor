Title
SequenceCompressor — consensus-labeled work segments

Notes / Description
SequenceCompressor turns a public ordered list into assignable work segments. First time: open the linked StudioNet contract, connect a test wallet, and call compile_sequence with a unique key, labels_json (for example ["Preparation","Execution","Verification"]), entries_json (2–30 short steps), and a policy explaining how to label each step. Validators agree on one closed label index per entry; the contract deterministically merges adjacent equal labels. Read get_compilation and get_segment. The owner assigns each segment to a distinct wallet, can reassign an unacknowledged segment, and seals after all current operators acknowledge. This records a workflow, not proof of completed work; it handles no funds. Oct 8 audit: lint/typecheck, 11 direct tests, five-validator GLSim, and full StudioNet compile-to-seal lifecycle with exact deployed-source match.
